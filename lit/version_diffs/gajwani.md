# Gajwani & Zhang
versions: IFPRI DP 00807 (Oct 2008), "Gender, Caste, and Public Goods Provision in Indian Village Governments" → World Bank PRWP 6854 (May 2014), "Gender and Public Goods Provision in Tamil Nadu's Village Governments" → WBER 29(2): 234–261 (2015; Advance Access 27 Mar 2014, so it predates the WPS)
bottom_line: The headline flips. The 2008 DP says women in reserved GPs "perform very similarly to men" and credits husbands' help for that. The 2014/2015 version says women-led GPs built fewer schools and roads (−0.840*** schools, +0.106* km to road with covariates) and credits women's low knowledge and weak contact with officials. Behind the flip: the women-reserved sample was recoded from 33 to 47 GPs after a "data entry error" was found, the outcomes were redefined, a GP fixed-effects specification with different covariates replaced the old one, and the caste half of the paper was cut. The 2014 WPS is the WBER text almost word for word.

## Unchanged (verified)
- Setting and design: 144 GPs, 12 blocks/PUCs × 12 GPs, 3 districts (Coimbatore, Pudukottai, Vellore) chosen by HDI tier; IFPRI–TNAU survey (2008 p.9; 2015 p.241).
- DID comparison of 1991 vs 2005. Toilets and bus data come from 1995 and 2004 (2008 p.11 fn16; 2015 p.245 fn18).
- Description of Tamil Nadu's ordered-ratio reservation rule (steps 1–5) and the 10-year reservation (2008 p.5; 2015 p.239).
- Citizen preference survey of 270 citizens in 27 villages, asked to list up to five infrastructure needs (2008 p.13; 2015 p.250).
- Block-level female/SC ratio table: same blocks, values differ only slightly (e.g., Aranthangi female ratio 0.5091 → 0.506) (2008 p.6; 2015 p.240).
- The anecdote that female presidents complained training came too late or not at all (2008 p.20; 2015 p.258).
- Women have lower knowledge-test scores and less contact with higher officials. The direction is the same in all versions; only the interpretation flips.
- 2014 WPS vs 2015 WBER: abstract, body text, Tables 1–8, Figure 1, and Appendices A1–A2 are identical apart from the copyedits listed. All coefficients I spot-checked match exactly (Table 5 col 6 −3.199 (0.474); Table 7 schools −0.693***; Table 8 schools −0.840***, road 0.106*).

## Caveats
- 2008 page numbers are the printed numbers (= PDF page − 7). WPS numbers are printed (= PDF page − 2). WBER numbers are printed (= PDF page + 233).
- WBER Advance Access is dated 27 Mar 2014, before the WPS (May 2014). So the WPS is a later reissue of the accepted article, not an intermediate draft. The "previous version" cited in WBER fn 20 (reservation data-entry error) and fn 24 (top-3 preference coding) matches the 2008 DP's 33-GP count and top-3 coding. That match is my inference; the footnotes do not name the DP.
- The coefficient comparisons are not like-for-like. The reserved/unreserved classification changed (33 → 47 GPs), several outcomes were redefined (HH composite → two counts; streetlight composite → operational count; health composite without MMR), and the specification changed (pooled OLS with district dummies → GP FE with test-score and BDO-contact controls, clustered SEs). Each of these shifts the estimates, and the documents do not separate their contributions. The recoding of reservation status is the most consequential, and it cannot be checked from the PDFs.
- WBER Table 8 controls for test score and BDO contact, which are plausibly downstream of gender (mediators). The 2008 covariate spec does not.
- In the PDF text layer of WBER tables, minus signs are rendered as "2" (e.g., "20.840***" = −0.840***). I read them as minus signs, consistent with the WPS.
- The 2008 Table 9 column order was inferred from header fragments and cross-checked against the text's description of which goods AIADMK and #villages predict. The order matches.
- 2008 text says "block-level control variables" (p.16), but Table 9 shows only Vellore and Pudukottai district dummies. That is an internal inconsistency in the DP.
- WBER supplemental appendix (Tables S1.x, S2, S3) was not available as a separate file. The S-table citations come from the WPS, which includes them.
