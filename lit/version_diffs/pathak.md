# Pathak & Macours
versions: HAL author manuscript, April 2016 (30 pdf pp.; printed = pdf − 1) → Economic Development and Cultural Change 65(4), 2017, pp. 741–766 (26 pdf pp.; printed = pdf + 740)
bottom_line: Substantively the same paper. Every number in Tables 1–10 matches, and so do the sample, design, outcomes and conclusions. What changed is journal copyediting: the abstract is dropped, sections are renumbered, footnotes shift by −1, a few citations are corrected, and Table 7 has small label edits.

## Unchanged (verified)
- I extracted the numeric tokens from Tables 1, 2 and 3 in both PDFs and diffed them: WP pdf 24/25/26 vs pub pdf 10/11/13 (pp. 750/751/753). They match except for thousand separators and page numbers.
- Tables 4–10: I compared every reservation coefficient, SE, star, N, R² and control mean by reading the two extractions side by side. WP pdf 27–30; pub pdf 16–21 (pp. 756–761). All identical. Headline Table 4 (in utero cohort, 1995 vs 2006 GPs): avg test z .188** (.085); HAZ .258** (.12); stunting −.064 (.046). Table 5: 2007 stunting −.127** (.060); 2002 vaccinations .182** (.080). Table 6: midday meal −.126*** (.045). Table 8 (6–11-month cohort): all n.s., avg z −.008 (.061). Table 9: 2007 HAZ .157* (.086), rest n.s. Table 10 (2001 vs 2006): 12–17 m avg z −.135 (.094); 6–11 m avg z .167** (.065), HAZ .238* (.12), stunting −.117* (.060).
- Table 8 keeps all eight outcome columns in both versions (the scrambled OCR suggested at first that two columns were missing; they are not).
- Sample is the same in both: Young Lives younger cohort, 1,547 children aged 6–17 months in 2002; 1,201 in mandals with election data; 384/240/577 in GPs reserved in 1995/2001/2006; for the 12–17-month group, 225/159/301. Balance and attrition text is the same (4% attrition, p = .43; 59 GPs split 20/12/27). WP fn 14 (pdf 11) vs pub fn 13 (p. 752).
- Design is the same in both: random rotation of reservation, comparing 1995-reserved with 2006-reserved GPs (and 2001 with 2006), the same controls, mandal FE, monthly-age FE and GP-clustered SEs. The equation and the control list are unchanged.
- Outcome set is the same in both: five EGRA/PPVT-type test scores plus their average, HAZ, stunting, vaccinations (BCG, measles, polio), and schooling indicators.
- Text in the body is word-for-word the same apart from copyedits: magnitude comparisons (.19 SD ≈ half a grade or 2.5 years of maternal schooling; vs .15 SD in Barham, Macours & Maluccio 2013; Jamaica 0.6 SD), the caution on the 2001 comparison, and the conclusion section (WP pdf 16 vs pub pdf 21–22, pp. 761–762).
## Caveats
- The published PDF is a scanned page image with an OCR text layer, so pub numbers were read from OCR, where spacing is garbled (e.g. "- .016", "199S"). Tables 4–10 were checked by reading them, not by an automated diff. For Tables 1–3 the automated numeric diff came back clean.
- The text comparison used a normalised word-level diff (case, punctuation and quote style stripped, "paper"→"article"). A change shorter than a few words that the OCR mangled could still have been missed. Every flagged span in the body was reviewed.
- The published PDF carries a reader's highlight on p. 747; this is not part of the article.
- Online appendices or supplementary material (if any) were not compared.
