# Bose & Das
versions: "Women's Reservation and India's National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme", preliminary draft, 17 Sep 2014 (pdf pp. 1–40) → "Political reservation for women and delivery of public works program", Review of Development Economics 22(1), 2018, pp. 203–219
bottom_line: The paper was re-estimated (OLS with block fixed effects and population-level controls → "random effects" with block and year fixed effects, share controls and caste-reservation controls; a different works panel) and several headline conclusions flip. The draft's role-model finding (women's person-days +6%*) and its "only newly reserved GPs" pattern for job cards and demand become no role-model effect and "only twice-reserved GPs". The draft's null on women-relevant works becomes +7% water control/harvesting, and the draft's irrigation and land-development gains disappear. The RDE version adds new SC/ST-targeting and works-per-rupee results.

## Unchanged (verified)
- Same nine districts: Barabanki, Chitrakoot, Fatehpur, Hardoi, Kheri (Phase I) and Ambedkar Nagar, Basti, Etah, Jhansi (Phase II) (draft p.16; RDE p.208).
- Separate Phase I and Phase II estimates, and the full / not-reserved-2005 / reserved-2005 column split (draft T3A–6B; RDE T4–6).
- Identification rests on the 2005→2010 reservation test, and both versions read it as a null with "ambiguous" sign (draft p.17; RDE p.210–211).
- Standard errors clustered at the Panchayat level (draft table notes; RDE table notes).
- Outcomes are in logs for demand, employment and works counts (draft headers/text; RDE T4–5).
- Reservation shares in Table 1: 2005 0.43/0.50, 2010 0.31/0.33 (draft T1 p.31; RDE T1 p.209).
- Phase I/II job-card and demand nulls in Phase II (draft p.18; RDE p.212).
- Person-days defined as total person-days over employed households (draft p.19; RDE p.212).
- UP background facts: 23/18/32 districts by phase, CAG Rs 22,714 crore, 18–20% women's participation, 14.5% decline (draft p.9–10; RDE p.207).
- The Afridi et al. (2013) Andhra Pradesh comparison, as the only prior NREGA-reservation paper (draft p.11; RDE p.205).

## Caveats
- I compared full text from pdftotext. I rendered only draft p.35 (Table 4A) to check the swapped column header. N=7534 in col 2 matches the "not reserved in 2005" N in Table 3A, so I read draft col 2 as not-reserved. If the header is right and the notes are wrong, the draft's 0.073* belongs to reserved-in-2005 GPs, and the "persistence" rows would partly un-flip. The draft text (p.21, "especially in those that did not have a woman leader in 2005") supports my reading.
- The changes in estimator, controls, year FE and works panel all happened at once, so the reversals cannot be attributed to any single change. "Random effects" with block FE and a time-invariant regressor is unusual, and the RDE gives no Hausman test or other justification (p.208).
- Internal inconsistencies in the RDE. Demand 0.028 with SE 0.013 (t≈2.15) is starred * rather than **. The p.205 and p.216 "no role-model effect" claim conflicts with p.213 "economically significant". The SC/ST claim rests on the works-per-rupee ratio (T6), not on work counts (T5, null).
- The units of person-days changed: draft T1 has 3.58 / 28.9, RDE T2 has 583.7 / 5930.4, which look like totals rather than per-household figures. The definitions may not match even though the text is identical.
- The draft's "Water Works" (T6A/B) and the RDE's "Water control and harvesting" may be different MIS categories. I treat the water result as a reversal of the conclusion, not of a like-for-like coefficient. The draft's T1 also lists a separate "Water Conservation Works" (0.66/1.12) that is never regressed.
- The draft's Table 3A/3B notes are truncated ("to control for any within"). The draft is marked "Preliminary ... do not cite".
- Renumbering: draft T2→RDE T3; T3A/3B+T4A/4B→T4 (Phase I/II side by side); T5A/5B + the water part of T6A/6B→T5; RDE T2 (means by reservation) and T6 (works/expenditure) are new. Draft Figures 1–3 dropped. The phase district list moved from fn16 (p.9) to Appendix B, and Appendix A (work types) is new. The RDE's equations are unnumbered although the text cites "Equation (1)".
