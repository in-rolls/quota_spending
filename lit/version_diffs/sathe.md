# Sathe, Klasen, Priebe & Biniwale
versions: CRC-PEG Discussion Paper 138 (April 2013; it calls itself "a version of" the EPW article) → EPW 48(11), 16 March 2013, pp. 50–57
bottom_line: The numbers in Tables 1–4 and 6 are identical and the conclusions are the same. One significance label differs: Table 5 row 5 (male-sarpanch villages, IODP in 2005 vs 2007) reads "Significant" in the DP but "Not significant" in EPW. The DP label matches the reported means and SDs (z ≈ 5.3) and the EPW label does not. The other differences are a softer abstract and copyedits.

## Unchanged (verified)
- Sample and design: Sangli district; survey Oct–Nov 2008; 32 villages (16 with female, 16 with male sarpanch); female-sarpanch villages drawn with probability proportional to size by taluka; each matched to a geographically close open-category male-sarpanch village; population > 600; 22 villages with 2005 elections and 10 with 2007 elections; 10 BPL + 10 APL women per village, so 640 respondents (DP pdf p5–7; EPW p51–52).
- IOSA described as eight items (Appendix A has 20 questions); IODP Appendix B has 18 questions; Appendix C coding is 0–1, with the gutters example closed = 1, open = 0.5, none = 0. The question lists are the same, and both versions head Appendix B "Questions asked for IOSA" (an error carried into EPW) (DP pdf p14–16; EPW p57).
- Methods: z-tests on group means, and OLS on respondent-level IOSA and IODP with Gen×Elect05 and Gen×IODP interactions; regression equations and variable definitions identical. Table 6 note "one-tailed test" in both (DP pdf p11–12, 21; EPW p55).
- Tables 1–3: every mean and SD identical. "Sample size 30 30" appears in all three tables in both versions, although there are 16+16 villages and 320+320 respondents (DP pdf p16–18; EPW p52–53).
- Table 4 (IOSA): rows 1–6 identical in means, SDs, Ns and significance (46.70/46.78 NS; 49.46/46.73 S; 40.64/46.89 S; 48.09/43.76 S; 46.73/46.89 NS; 49.46/40.64 S). The stray "4.c.2" label in row 3 is in both (DP pdf p19–20; EPW p54).
- Table 5 (IODP), rows 1–4 and 6: identical. Row 1 reports female-village N = 220 rather than 320 in both. Row 3 (2007: female 26.99 < male 30.72, Significant) is in both, as is the text describing it, which contradicts the conclusion that women's participation is higher in female-sarpanch villages "for elections held one year back and 3½ years back both" (DP pdf p10, 13; EPW p54, 56–57).
- Table 6: all 5 columns identical, every coefficient and star, plus Adj. R² of 0.192, 0.202, 0.212, 0.305, 0.334 (DP pdf p21; EPW p55).
- The "49.6" in the text versus 49.46 in Table 4 appears in both versions.
- Conclusions, policy recommendation (lengthen reservation from 5 to 10 years) and footnotes 1–5 are the same in substance.

## Caveats
- The published version was compared through pdftotext (its two-column layout scrambles the text order) together with rendered images of printed pp. 52–55. I checked the tables against the images, not only the extracted text. The DP tables were checked through layout text plus a rendered pdf p21.
- I compared the prose paragraph by paragraph for substance, not word by word. Small copyedits beyond those listed (for example "sarpanchs" → "sarpanch", "per cent" → "%") are not itemised.
- The Table 5 row 5 z-statistic is my own recomputation as a two-sample z-test from the reported means, SDs and Ns, treating respondents as independent. Clustering by village would shrink it, but very probably not below 1.96.
- The DP is dated April 2013, after the EPW issue, and describes itself as a version of the article. "Working → published" is therefore not a strict chronological order; the DP is more likely the authors' manuscript posted afterwards.
