# Ban & Rao
versions: Ban & Rao, "Tokenism or Agency? The Impact of Women's Reservations on Village Democracies in South India," World Bank working paper, May 2007 (38 pp.) → Ban & Rao (2008), Economic Development and Cultural Change 56(3): 501–530
bottom_line: The headline results do not change. Tables 1 and 3–15 and the appendix tables have the same coefficients, SEs and stars, and so do the text claims about them. The one re-estimated table is the Table 2 exogeneity (balance) check. It is now run on the 106-GP analysis sample rather than 183–188 of 192 GPs, so every marginal effect changes and a second covariate (irrigated land) becomes significant. The conclusion's AP/state-maturity claim is also softened and its policy sentence rewritten.

## Unchanged (verified)
- Title and authors are the same. Rao's affiliation is shortened from "Development Research Group, World Bank" to "World Bank" (p.1 / p.501).
- Sample and data are the same: 9 districts in AP/KA/KE/TN; 201 GPs, 527 villages, 5,180 households; 192 presidents; analysis sample of 106 GPs, 310 villages, ~2,100 HH and ~7,100 individuals; survey Nov–Dec 2002; PRA, facilities, household and president instruments (pp.5–8 / pp.505–507).
- Design is the same: block FE; block-pair FE for state interactions with Kerala omitted; SEs clustered at the GP level; LPM for facilities outcomes; equations (1)–(4) (pp.12–16 / pp.517–525).
- Table 1 is identical: 27 reserved and 79 unreserved GPs; 15.2% female presidents in unreserved GPs (p.22 / p.508).
- Table 3 is identical: gram sabha participation .0270 (.0180) (p.24 / p.510).
- Table 4 is identical. Education .184 (.089)**, the only significant PRA outcome; PRA joint test p = .392, facilities p = .876; met higher panchayat −.202 (.078)** (p.25 / p.512).
- Tables 5–8 are identical, including Table 7 spouse 5 vs 1 (18.52% vs 1.27%) and the Table 8 means and SDs (pp.26–29 / pp.514–516).
- Table 9 is identical, rendered and checked. Examples: education vs unreserved −2.620 (.766)***; political experience −.461 (.188)**. The same carried-over star omissions appear in both versions: knowledge col 2 −1.413 (.292) and wealthy col 3 .317 (.050) have no stars (p.30 / p.518).
- Table 10 is identical: reservation × experience .268**, reservation main effect −.358* (p.31 / p.521). Checked from OCR.
- Table 11 is identical, rendered and checked: reservation × experience .533***, × young .912**, × land .017*, president wealthy .178* (p.32 / p.522).
- Table 12 is identical, rendered and checked: reservation .157**, × upper-caste land −.367** (p.33 / p.523).
- Table 13 is identical: × Gini high −.579*** (p.34 / p.524). Checked from OCR.
- Table 14 is identical, rendered and checked: × AP −1.074***, × KA .108, × TN −.215 (p.35 / p.525).
- Table 15 is identical: reservation −.490**, × AP .539*, × KA .577* (p.36 / p.526). Checked from OCR.
- Annex 1–3 are identical to Tables A1–A3; A1 was rendered and checked (pp.37–38 / pp.528–529).
- Main conclusions are kept: no tokenism through weak-women selection or spouses; no average performance difference; women do worse only on meeting higher officials; experience × reservation is positive; upper-caste land × reservation is negative; no CD-style preference alignment (pp.16–17 / pp.526–527).
- Renumbering: sections 1–4 become I–IV, with 3.x subsections turned into lettered or unnumbered subheads. Footnotes 1–8 are unchanged; a new fn 9 (Table 2 probits) shifts all later footnotes by +1 (WP fn 20 becomes pub fn 21, WP 21 becomes pub 22). Annexes 1–3 become Tables A1–A3. Table numbers 1–15 are unchanged.

## Caveats
- For the published version, Tables 1, 3–8, 10, 13 and 15, the A2/A3 values, and the published footnotes 18–20 were checked against OCR only, not rendered. The OCR matched the WP digits apart from obvious garbling of signs and decimal points (for example "-500" for .500 in Table 10).
- I did not render the published equations (2) and (4) because the OCR dropped them. Equation (3) was rendered (p.522) and matches the WP form.
- The paper does not say why Table 2 was re-run on the 106-GP sample. I am inferring that it was to match the analysis sample; this is not stated.
- The WP is a text PDF, so its numbers come from pdftotext and are reliable.
